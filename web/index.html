Point-in-time capture of public job postings; aggregates only, nothing personal. Regions are derived from free-text locations, best effort and in public in app.js: "Unrecognised" means no known pattern matched, "Remote (unspecified)" means the posting says remote without naming a region. Greenhouse reports a posting's updated date rather than its first-published date, so a few dormant postings carry old dates. Age in the freshness section is measured from your clock against the snapshot's posted dates; the snapshot refreshes daily, so the two normally agree within a day. *Counts remote roles whose recognised region is not the United States.
